Data attribution

Historical ball data: supplied CSV export of Cricsheet matches (IPL, T20I and
Asia Cup tournaments, 2022-26). Original source:
https://cricsheet.org/downloads/
https://cricsheet.org/format/json/

The underlying Cricsheet data is made available under the Open Data Commons Attribution License (ODC-By) v1.0:
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1-0/

Rankings are based on the matches in the supplied CSV, using the ranking method
described in the repository README. Derived summaries are transformed analysis
outputs, not original Cricsheet delivery files.

Bowling historical reporting labels: aadi-jn/indian-premier-league, joined by registry ID for reporting only, as recorded in the supplied analysis provenance:
https://github.com/aadi-jn/indian-premier-league/blob/main/docs/data_dictionary.md

Team and style labels are historical. They do not establish present team, retention status or auction eligibility.
