READ THE SCORES

Performance, with context.

These are model estimates from the same final pressure-only analysis used on the leaderboards, including the wider modelled pool. They are historical scores, not predictions or official RR ratings.

Squad membership does not imply final-shortlist eligibility. Scores below the final-list floors (550 batting / 601 bowling pressure balls) remain visible here as squad context. Small samples are marked provisional. Open a card's score details for its pressure-ball count and 80% conditional interval.

Intervals omit model-form and calibration uncertainty. Unavailable means no observed score in the supplied dataset, never a score of zero.
