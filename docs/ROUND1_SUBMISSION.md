# Round 1 submission checklist

The goal is historical performance under pressure, not an RR retention or auction-buy recommendation. The pressure definition and selection rationale are part of the result.

## Required package

- Ten ordered batters and ten ordered bowlers, with no duplicate within a list.
- The same order in the report and Unstop's 20 required player dropdowns.
- A methodology report covering pressure definition, performance versus own baseline, fair comparisons, sample and activity rules, IPL/T20I context, evidence for the selections, consistency and limitations.
- The live form accepts PDF/PPTX and has a dashboard-link field. A short PDF/PPT including the dashboard link avoids relying on ambiguous dashboard-only upload mechanics.

Journey content is optional. The squad planner is not a Round 1 dependency. No particular model is required.

## Explain the selection funnel

The default lists use at least 60 pressure and 200 normal legal balls, recent activity, a 35-player shortlist on normal and overall skill, non-negative pressure performance, and ordering by shrunk pressure-minus-normal uplift. This gate is designed to avoid rewarding a weak baseline alone. It is a methodological choice, not an organizer eligibility rule. The batting 3/4/3 role-balanced list is supplementary, not the default submission list.

Do not describe exact rank differences as certain. Preserve the stated bootstrap scope, weak cross-match consistency and missing-input reproducibility caveat. This is historical analysis, not future prediction.

## Release boundary

The 17 November code-only correction pass does not check or alter data or rankings. A final report must be reconciled with the approved existing lists before submission. Code corrections are not evidence that the published data was rerun. Recover the existing method PDF before deciding how much report revision is needed. Nothing is submitted automatically.
